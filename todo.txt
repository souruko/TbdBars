
- resizing a bar reflows the row-major slot index, so shortcuts shift position,
  and narrowing orphans the entries past the new slot count (never restored,
  never deleted, reappear if the bar is widened again). needs a key scheme that
  survives a reflow, which means a save migration.

- DE/FR clients mangle saved shortcut ids: ConvertFromEuro runs every scalar
  through tonumber, and tonumber("0x70003F0E") is 1879064334, so quickslot
  assignments likely do not survive a reload on a german or french client.
